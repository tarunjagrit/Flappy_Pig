# Flappy Pig 🐷

A small Flappy Bird-style game I made while learning JavaScript.

The idea is pretty simple: you control a pig, click to make it fly, and try to get through the pipes without crashing.
https://tarunjagrit.github.io/Flappy_Pig/

## What is different?

Well... it's a pig.

Flappy Bird has a bird.

This has a pig.

And there is one very important difference:

**The pig can stand on the ground.**

Why?

Because it's a pig. 🐷

## How to play

- Click anywhere on the game to make the pig go up.
- Don't hit the pipes.
- Don't fall.
- Try to survive as long as possible.
